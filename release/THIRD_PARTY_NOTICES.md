# Third-party components in the UIcore JAR

> **Language:** English | [Русская версия](THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES_RU.md)

UIcore includes MorePaperLib classes under GNU LGPL v3 or later. The included source and rebuild instructions are in [morepaperlib-source](morepaperlib-source/README.md). LGPL v3 and GPL v3 copies are in `third-party/` and in `META-INF/licenses/` inside the JAR.

UIcore also includes RE2/J 1.8 for safe regular-expression processing. Its notice is in `META-INF/licenses/RE2J-LICENSE.txt` inside the JAR and in [third-party/RE2J-LICENSE.txt](third-party/RE2J-LICENSE.txt). Source: [google/re2j](https://github.com/google/re2j).

The source contains bStats Metrics classes. They do not start telemetry in this build. The MIT notice is in `META-INF/licenses/BSTATS-METRICS-LICENSE.txt` and [third-party/BSTATS-METRICS-LICENSE.txt](third-party/BSTATS-METRICS-LICENSE.txt). Source: [Bastian/bstats-metrics](https://github.com/Bastian/bstats-metrics).

UIcore is based on [CustomScreenMenu](https://builtbybit.com/resources/customscreenmenu.108929/) by wuhaoxian123 and is published with the author's permission.
